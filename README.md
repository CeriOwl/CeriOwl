## 🚀 Selected Projects

### Citizen Services Case Management Platform
`Next.js` `Supabase` `Docker` `Google Cloud` `Python` `FastAPI` `Nginx`

An internal platform that replaced a Google Sheets workflow for managing citizen reports received through social media. It centralizes incident details, locations, categories, and follow-up status, includes case-tracking workflows and a reporting dashboard with downloadable charts, and has a reusable library of public-facing responses. A FastAPI image service serves low-, medium-, and high-resolution variants from a Synology NAS behind Nginx.
**Used by 15 people across two teams.**

### Google Sheets Data Synchronization Service *(in development)*
`Express.js` `Better Auth` `Google Sheets API` `PostgreSQL` `Redis` `BullMQ` `Docker Compose`

A backend service that mirrors Google Sheets data into PostgreSQL. It uses range-based retrieval and pagination to process data in chunks, polling with Redis caching to detect updates, and BullMQ queues for synchronization and reconciliation.

### FIFA Fan Festival Nuevo León: Landing Page
`Astro` `Tailwind CSS`

Independently developed the landing page for the event's public-facing web presence.

### Agricultura Regenerativa by Don Julio: Website
`React` `Tailwind CSS`

A website for a regenerative agriculture initiative focused on sustainability and educational webinars.
